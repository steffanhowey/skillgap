# CPO Decisions — Unblock and Build

**Date:** 2026-08-28  
**Role:** Chief Product Officer, incoming  
**Status:** In force. Supersedes conflicting gates where named below.  
**Owner:** Product / founder

---

## The situation in one paragraph

SkillGap has a strong vision, several good subsystems, and a 3.6/10 delivered product. The team then did the right thing: audited honestly, wrote a validation PRD, then a production PRD. The wrong thing happened next. Those documents were stacked as a **serial freeze**. Nothing production-shaped can start until 30 blinded comparisons finish. That is research discipline applied as a build stop. It is why the company feels stuck.

The actual product a user can use today is still: five editorial Marketing missions, room-required, labeled as market demand, and a profile that says capability is proven after a short one-step task. The Message Review lab is a good experiment. It is not the product.

---

## The calls

### D1. For the next six weeks, SkillGap is one product

A B2B SaaS Product Marketing Manager with a real launch in the next seven days practices an evidence-to-message workflow and leaves with work they can use.

Everything else is inventory, infrastructure, or later. We will not expand functions, catalog, rooms, labs, or credentials until this wedge is honest and complete.

### D2. Split the Stage 2 gate. Do not treat it as a build freeze.

`PRD_Real_Work_Value_Loop.md` Stage 2 still stands for **value claims and scale**. It no longer freezes all production work.

| Still gated on Stage 2 | Unblocked now |
|------------------------|---------------|
| “Better than ChatGPT” | Truthful copy |
| Public proficiency / verified skill | Solo completion of existing missions |
| Catalog cutover to a new wedge | Schema ledger reconciliation |
| Fluency advancement from this wedge | Private, allowlisted foundation for the wedge |
| Generalized artifact platform | Dated editorial source packet for “why now” |
| Room-required learning | Continuing the lab study in parallel |

**Rationale:** The validation PRD forbade production infrastructure so we would not gold-plate a hypothesis. That intent is correct for public claims. It is incorrect for stopping us from removing product lies and giving users a way to do the work without a room.

### D3. Three tracks, in parallel, not a queue

**Track T — Truth (this week).** Remove unsupported claims. Make solo the primary way to do a mission. Rooms become optional.

**Track V — Validation (founder-owned recruiting).** Run lab Stage 1 (8–10), then Stage 2 (30) exactly as written. Do not invest more lab UI unless a study-blocking bug appears.

**Track F — Foundation (allowlisted, private).** After Track T lands, start the wedge spine in `PRD_Evidence_Backed_Launch_Messaging.md`: attempts, artifacts, server evaluation, AI gateway. No public receipts. No catalog replacement. Feature-flagged.

### D4. The intelligence engine is not a ship blocker

Do not restart the full signal/cluster/market-state pipeline as a prerequisite for the wedge. Use a dated, editorially approved source packet. Say “editorial next step,” never “market demand,” until `fp_skill_market_state` has a live row.

### D5. The lab is a study. The product is a learning path.

Reuse the lab’s evaluator patterns. Do not promote `/labs/message-review` into the product center. Do not add lab surfaces.

### D6. Rooms are optional social context

The same mission attempt can later enter a room. Rooms do not own start, progress, or completion. If a room is unavailable, the user can still finish.

### D7. Language is a product object

Until calibrated:

- “Capability practiced,” never “capability proven”
- “AI-reviewed against supplied criteria,” never “verified proficiency”
- “Editorial next step,” never “market demand” without market state
- “Checked against the proof you supplied,” never independent fact-checking

### D8. 8/10 is earned by the wedge, not declared for the platform

The production PRD scorecard still applies. We do not say SkillGap is 8/10. We say this wedge is 8/10 only after technical gates, Stage 2, and the 12–20 user pilot all pass.

## What we will not do

- Write another strategy or PRD before shipping Track T
- Add missions, functions, or rooms as the next body of work
- Expand the Message Review lab as a product
- Claim currentness, market demand, or proven skill
- Wait for 30 study participants before a user can complete a mission alone

---

## What we build this week (Track T)

1. Fix dishonest copy and recommendation reasons.
2. Ship a solo mission player using the existing Watch / Do / Check / Reflect renderers.
3. Make **Start** / **Continue** the primary CTA. Room is secondary.
4. Keep the five launch missions as the current inventory. Do not add a sixth.

Track F starts only after Track T is usable end to end.

If a task does not make the PMM wedge more truthful, more completable, or more measurable, it is out of scope.
